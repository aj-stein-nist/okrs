# BTF Full Semiconductor Traceability

- What? Define MVP elements for tracing hardware and get industry adoption.
- How now? Specialists manually do traceability checks repetitively in private. 
- What's new? Industry early adopters pushing for transparency services in SDOs.
- Who cares? Manufacturer supply chain and customer acquisition specialists
- How much? $50,000 for research
- How long? 1 year
- Milestones? Lifecycle; actors inventory; MVP data elements inventory; interaction models; example traceability dataset
